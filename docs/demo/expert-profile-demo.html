Summary

Jordan Sample, Ph.D., P.E. (a fictional demo expert) is a biomedical engineer in Boston who analyzes why orthopedic implants and surgical devices fail. He is a principal at Example Forensic Engineering and was a senior R&D engineer at Northwind Orthopedics from 2012 to 2018. He has testified mostly for device makers: 18 depositions and 4 trials since 2016, about 80% for defendants. In Roe v. Northwind Orthopedics, a court barred part of his testimony as outside his expertise.
